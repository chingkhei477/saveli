Offers

Cashback offers from Saveli partner stores

Each offer shows the cashback rate, the expected tracking and confirmation time, and the main conditions, so you can decide before you visit the store.

Current offers

Rates and conditions are set by each store and can change. The rate shown when you click through is the one we apply, unless the store reports your order under a different category.

What every offer tells you

Read these details before you shop. They come from the store’s own programme terms.

Cashback rate
A percentage of the eligible order value, or a fixed rupee amount. “Up to” means the highest rate applies only to some categories.
Tracking time
How long the store usually takes to report an order to Saveli after you pay.
Confirmation time
How long the store usually takes to approve cashback, which follows its return and cancellation window.
Conditions and exclusions
Products, payment methods, coupon codes or order types that do not earn cashback at that store.

Tracking tips

Help your order track

Most missing cashback comes from a tracking link that was interrupted. These steps reduce that risk.

  • Click through from Saveli immediately before you shop, and pay in the same visit.
  • Allow cookies for the store and pause ad blockers for that visit.
  • Use only coupon codes that the Saveli offer lists or allows.
  • Do not visit another cashback, coupon or comparison site between clicking and paying.
