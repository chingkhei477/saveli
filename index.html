Cashback for online shoppers in India

Get part of your money back when you shop online.

When you visit a partner store through Saveli and place an order, the store pays us a commission. We share that commission with you as cashback, and show you each step until it reaches your UPI ID or bank account.

See how it works Browse offers

Cashback depends on the store confirming your order. Orders that are cancelled or returned do not earn cashback.

What happens after you shop

  1. Order trackedTracked

    The store reports your order to Saveli through its affiliate partner.

  2. Store checks the orderPending

    Cashback waits while the return and cancellation window runs.

  3. Store approves itConfirmed

    The store confirms the sale and pays its commission.

  4. Money reaches youPaid

    You withdraw confirmed cashback to your UPI ID or bank account.

In four steps

How Saveli cashback works

  1. Pick an offer

    Choose a partner store on Saveli and read its cashback rate and conditions.

  2. Shop as usual

    Click through to the store from Saveli and complete your order in the same visit.

  3. Store confirms

    Your cashback shows as Pending until the store’s return window ends and it approves the order.

  4. Withdraw

    Move confirmed cashback to your UPI ID or bank account in your own name.

Before you start

Good to know

Cashback is a share of a store’s commission, so the store’s rules decide whether an order qualifies. These points cover most questions.

Read the Cashback Terms

  • Start every order from Saveli, and finish it in the same browser without switching to another cashback or coupon site.
  • Confirmation takes time. Stores wait until their return and cancellation window has passed before they approve an order.
  • Cancelled, returned or exchanged orders, and orders that break the store’s offer conditions, do not earn cashback.
  • Cashback is not income or a guaranteed return. It is a partial refund on purchases you already chose to make.
