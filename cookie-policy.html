Cookie Policy

Which cookies Saveli uses, and why partner stores use cookies to track your cashback.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. This policy explains which of these are used on saveli.in and by partner stores when you shop through Saveli.

Cookies on saveli.in

The public pages of saveli.in do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with them to download the font files. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

When you log in to your Saveli account, we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account. These cookies are essential for the service and cannot be switched off in our systems.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

Cookies used for cashback tracking

When you click through from Saveli to a partner store, the store or its affiliate network sets its own cookies or uses similar technology to record that your visit came from Saveli. This is how the store knows to pay commission for your order, and it is what makes cashback possible.

These cookies are controlled by the store or affiliate network, not by Saveli, and their own cookie and privacy policies apply. If you block them, your order may not track and you may not receive cashback.

Managing cookies

You can view, block and delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you allow cookies for specific sites. If you want your cashback to track, allow cookies for the store you are visiting and pause any ad blocker or privacy extension for that visit.
