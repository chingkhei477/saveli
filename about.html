About Saveli

A cashback service built on clear information

Saveli is an Indian cashback platform based in Moirang, Manipur. We help people in India get part of their spending back from online stores, and we explain every step in plain language.

How Saveli earns money

We think you should know exactly how a cashback service is paid.

Online stores run affiliate programmes. They pay a commission to websites that send them customers who place valid orders. Saveli joins these programmes, usually through affiliate networks, and receives a commission when you shop through a Saveli link.

We keep part of that commission to run Saveli, and pass the rest to you as cashback. The store sets the commission, so rates differ between stores and product categories, and they can change.

Because the money comes from the store, cashback is only paid when the store confirms and pays for an order. Saveli does not add a charge to the price you pay at the store.

How we work

No exaggerated promises

Cashback is a partial refund on purchases, not a way to earn income. We do not advertise guaranteed amounts.

Terms you can read

Care with your data
